# Bank-Customer-Churn-Analysis
An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing loan default risk using SQL and Power BI.

The project explores customer demographics, credit scores, account information, financial characteristics, employment, and other customer attributes to identify patterns associated with customer churn.
The objective is to transform raw banking customer data into meaningful business insights that can help stakeholders understand which customer segments have higher churn rates and where customer retention may require further attention.

📌 Business Problem
Customer retention is an important business challenge for banks. Understanding why customers leave and which customer segments have higher churn rates can help organizations make better-informed retention decisions.
This project analyzes customer churn across different demographic, financial, and account-related characteristics.
The analysis focuses on identifying:
The overall customer churn rate
Churn patterns across credit score ranges
Churn by loan amount
Churn by debt-to-income ratio (DTI)
Churn by employment group
Churn by employment status
Churn by loan purpose
Churn by employment tenure
The results are presented through SQL analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard.
---

🎯 Project Objectives
The main objectives of this project are to:
Clean and prepare the raw banking customer data.
Analyze customer churn using SQL.
Calculate overall and segmented churn rates.
Identify customer segments with different observed churn rates.
Analyze relationships between customer characteristics and churn.
Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
Present findings in a clear, business-focused format.

📊 Business Questions
1. What is the overall customer churn rate?
Calculate the percentage of customers who have churned compared with the total customer population.
2. What is the churn rate by credit score range?
Analyze churn across different credit score groups, such as:
520–599
600–649
650–699
700–749
750+
3. What is the churn rate by loan amount?
Analyze whether customer churn differs across different loan amount groups.
4. What is the churn rate by DTI?
Analyze churn across different debt-to-income ratio groups.
5. What is the churn rate by employment group?
Compare churn rates across different employment/income groups.
6. What is the churn rate by employment status?
Analyze differences in churn between different employment statuses.
7. What is the churn rate by loan purpose?
Determine whether customer churn differs according to the purpose of the customer's loan.
8. What is the churn rate by employment tenure?
Analyze whether customer churn differs according to how long customers have been employed.
